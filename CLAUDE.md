# Agent rules for Constellation

These are hard rules for every agent and human writing in this repo. They override convenience and deadlines.

## How we talk about Archipelago

Archipelago is a volunteer-built open-source project, and its community is where our games, our early users and our goodwill come from. We are building on top of what they proved works. Treat the project and its people accordingly.

### MUST

1. **Be accurate.** Every claim about Archipelago's code, behavior or numbers must be checked against the code or docs, with a file and line or a link, or labeled **not verified** or **inferred**. Date-stamp snapshots. Archipelago changes, so recheck before anything is published.
2. **Critique design, never people.** Describe what a design costs a player or developer and why it was probably built that way. Acknowledge that it was built by volunteers, mostly for people comfortable with technical setup.
3. **Credit them.** Where we are compatible or learned from them, say so. Keep every required MIT copyright and permission notice with anything we reuse, and list it in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (create it when first needed).
4. **Frame as a complement.** In anything external, we are the easy front door that runs what they built, a compatible superset. We say "works with Archipelago" and "compatible with Archipelago".
5. **Report real defects upstream first.** If we confirm a bug in their code, tell the maintainers before we mention it anywhere else, and publish only after they have had a fair chance to respond.
6. **Get review before anything goes outside this repo.** Any document, post, deck or page that mentions Archipelago needs a read by a different agent against these rules and a human sign-off.

### NEVER

1. **Never insult.** No "crazy", "nightmare", "wild", "horrible", "archaic", "garbage", "broken by design", "anti-pattern" or similar, in any external text. Keep internal notes factual too. Words people use in chat are not a reason to repeat them in files.
2. **Never publish unconfirmed bug claims.** A suspected bug stays in internal notes, marked suspected, until reproduced and reported upstream.
3. **Never imply affiliation or endorsement.** No "official", "Archipelago 2", "Archipelago v2", "the new Archipelago", "next-gen Archipelago", or any name that could be mistaken for theirs. Use "compatible with Archipelago" only.
4. **Never use their logos, branding or art.** Parts of their site assets are marked "All rights reserved". Any asset we want to reuse needs its license checked first.
5. **Never name individual maintainers or contributors in criticism**, and never quote private chats or Discord messages.
6. **Never say "hostile fork", "replacement", "killer", "takeover" or "incumbent" in external text.** Internally, strategy notes may say "competing product" when discussing business, and must stay factual.
7. **Never copy their code, text, docs, tests or data into this repo without an explicit decision**, a license check and a notice entry. Describing what they do in our own words with citations is fine.
8. **Never present estimates as measurements.** Label every number as measured, counted, or estimated, and say how.

### Banned phrases (external text)

`crazy`, `nightmare`, `wild`, `horrible`, `archaic`, `anti-pattern`, `broken`, `garbage`, `hostile fork`, `replacement for Archipelago`, `Archipelago killer`, `incumbent`, `next-gen Archipelago`, `Archipelago 2`.

## Before you publish anything that mentions Archipelago

- [ ] Each claim has a source or a "not verified" label.
- [ ] No suspected bugs, and no unreported confirmed bugs.
- [ ] No banned phrase, no insult, no named individuals.
- [ ] Framed as compatible and complementary.
- [ ] No unlicensed asset or copied text.
- [ ] A different agent reviewed it against this file, and a human approved it.

## Other rules

- This is research, not legal advice. Anything about publishers, trademarks, or licensing in external text needs a lawyer's review first.
- Do not copy anything from the Archipelago repo into this one. Notes cite file paths in their repo, they do not reproduce its contents.
- No em dashes in text we write.
